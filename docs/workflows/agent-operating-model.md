# Agent Operating Model

Status: accepted workflow reference

This file owns lifecycle, change control, review/reuse, program economics and
agent evidence transport. Roles, safety/authority, fingerprint mechanics and
lane assignments have their own linked references; do not duplicate their
normative detail here. Owner-authorized amendments apply prospectively after
their documentation review; retain the bound revision of frozen candidates.

## First-Read And Context Rules

Start with repo-root `AGENTS.md`, then the narrow workflow, current-state,
source or explicitly named local roadmap needed. Use heading scans and
targeted searches before broad reads. Raw logs and protected evidence stay
behind concise pointers. Role Locks change at ownership/authority boundaries,
not at every tool call or technical clarification.

## Current Record And Evidence Transport

Maintain one discoverable current entry point per program. It may itself
contain the capsule and ledger, or point to their same-epoch views; three
physical files are not required. Replace current state on material changes.
Never rewrite historical verdicts or mutate stopped-owner artifacts; a later
authorized task publishes a forward supersession record instead.

Keep the active summary short enough to select the next action without reading
history. Archive superseded narrative once and link it; preserve required raw
evidence and historical verdicts in their original locations. Do not retain old
"current" or "next" instructions inside the active summary. A reviewable current
record is ordinary documentation, not a new executable receipt protocol.

Authorized verified archival under the worktree retention rules may relocate
evidence. Preserve its exact contents, original identities and verdicts, and
record a direct relocation reference; do not rewrite history or treat relocation
as new acceptance, readiness or execution authority.

The capsule carries objective, role/authority and stable IDs; source/target
identity and ownership; bound freeze; latest actual result; cumulative
controls; remaining gates; and next action. Aim for 300–500 words for complex
continuations; shorter is fine when the necessary facts fit. This is a context
budget, not a fill quota or executable gate.

The ledger records current gate/outcome dispositions, achieved delta,
candidate/input identity, cumulative initial/repair/environment consumption,
effort limits, unresolved findings/decisions, and next owner/action. Readiness
views can share this record. Keep actual saved outcomes distinct from source
acceptance, custody, performance and deployed behavior.

Store historical briefs, reviews, manifests and observations once, referenced
by exact identity and location. Do not embed previous ledgers, whole freezes,
outcome JSON or recursively expanded pin chains in the current record or
handoff. A flat current coverage/outcome row points directly to its original
evidence and latest relevant disposition. Do not walk or rehash all historical
dependencies unless they are relevant to the present claim. Prove current
selected inputs and reused evidence at admission; repeat verification on
relevant drift or where exact review identity requires it.

This is an agent-workflow transport rule. It neither compacts application
proof/history nor changes its runtime verification, data custody or audit
contract. No new status service, permanent Overseer or heartbeat is introduced.

## Proportionate Delivery

Choose the route by actual effects and dependencies, including later execution,
not file size or whether a script is private. Documentation keeps its existing
route. Changes to product behavior, application schema/migrations, runtime
configuration, authority, protected targets/actions or security/preservation
guarantees use the canonical product lifecycle, even inside a dormant helper or
receipt/admission schema. Canonical documentary changes keep their documentation
route and independent semantic review.

Bounded local tooling may use a compact preparation route when it changes only
development, capture or evidence mechanics while those contracts remain fixed,
and cannot perform a protected operation during preparation:

1. Record one concise brief in the existing current record: intended outcome,
   owned paths/inputs, permitted local effects, preserved requirements,
   acceptance, effort checkpoint and applicable remaining allowances. The
   Orchestrator may also be Scout and Implementer; approve the brief inline.
2. Keep implementation and focused compatibility checks mutable. Resolve the
   critical execution dependencies before assembly. Internal drafts and ordinary
   no-effect preparation failures need no new cycle, document or human approval.
3. Assemble once, identify the exact helper/input artifact, and obtain one
   independent technical review of the connected changed paths. Include owning
   runbook/contract semantics in that review; add no duplicate documentary pass.
   A post-assembly executable/semantic change still needs a successor candidate.

Keep the existing Program and cumulative controls. Classify helper candidates
separately from product-source candidates only when their effects are genuinely
separate; never reclassify a source fix or reset any existing allowance. Default
candidate limits apply unless matching authority states otherwise. A mechanical
adapter for an unchanged protected-operation contract still needs exact review
of its affected control paths before use. A change to that contract uses the
canonical route; preparation acceptance grants no protected execution.

Use the smallest adequate control: established operations, one concise runbook
and direct evidence references are the default. Explain any new dispatcher,
receipt schema, phase admission or hash-binding layer by its concrete requirement
and why existing mechanisms are insufficient. Separate required outcomes from
replaceable implementation choices. Do not add quotas for individual reads,
process queries or proof bookkeeping by default. An existing explicit cap or
approved security/preservation requirement remains binding.

Request only missing authority. One matching grant may cover implementation,
independent review and conditional execution within its stated bounds. Technical
acceptance, authorization and actual effects remain distinct, but need not each
become a human approval exchange or a custom executable gate.

## Coherent Slice Delivery

Use these defaults within the routes and controls above; use the existing
current record and review, without another document, receipt or approval layer.

- One accountable owner may combine Scout, Implementer and Orchestrator work.
  Prefer one available independent Verifier through a coherent slice; rotate
  at a verified boundary or for a concrete review gap, rather than adding a
  standing serial pass. Independence and complete applicable coverage remain.
- Keep ordinary preparation and compatibility feedback mutable before assembly.
  Trace the actual outer route registration, request/action branches, local
  facade and provider, save/transaction, postcommit confirmation and ordinary
  reader as one affected family. Check argument binding, raw value/return
  types, source/state phases and failure ownership, including affected siblings.
- Reuse unchanged review coverage under the rules below; identity alone does
  not establish continuing validity of its dependency assumptions.
- Prepare static expectations and reviewer availability before issuing an
  expiring proposal when possible. Accept the actual fresh proposal at its
  required boundary; preserve normal authentication, expiry and currentness.
- Parallelize distinct read-only source/evidence preparation when useful;
  serialize shared source, target, current-record and operational-session
  writes. Genuine application reads can have side effects and need their own
  scope analysis; they are not saved-artifact inspection.
- Use existing timestamps to distinguish preparation, requests, review wait
  and evidence comparison where observed. Leave unattributed time unknown;
  no new timing service, instrumentation or diagnostic gate follows.

Rotate long-running ownership at a verified slice boundary after reconciling
in-flight effects and owned resources. Replace the short current capsule and
link archived history; carry forward settled facts, completed writes that must
not replay, cumulative controls, remaining evidence limits and the next bounded
action. A fresh thread reads current governance separately from frozen authority
and receives no replenished allowance. Compaction alone establishes no defect
cause. Existing frozen helper hashing, intermediate captures, authentication,
freshness/CAS/reservation, recovery and terminal preservation remain binding;
changing their mechanisms requires a separately scoped prospective disposition.

## Discovery, Requirements And Mutable Implementation

Each product candidate-producing cycle has one accountable Scout, one approved
Requirements Freeze, one mutable implementation wave, one assembled candidate
and one independent exact-candidate review. Local tooling may use the compact
route above; documentation-only work uses its scoped route without invented
implementation IDs. Role separation is required for independent acceptance;
other roles may share one agent when ownership and authority permit.

Discovery may iterate within its bounded read-only scope before approval.
Reuse relevant prior diagnosis rather than restarting the entire audit.
The Scout brief and freeze name the complete supported journey and affected
siblings; acceptance and priorities; decisions/deferrals; dependencies and
compatibility; ownership/authority; non-goals and technical latitude; selected
toolchain/inputs; evidence/review scope; and finite candidate/effort limits.

A Requirements Freeze fixes requirements, not yet the implementation bytes.
Ordinary technical investigation, edits and useful focused feedback under
unchanged requirements/authority remain inside the mutable wave. A different
producer diagnosis alone does not require another Scout or consume a candidate.
A genuine requirement or acceptance change closes the cycle and needs the next
cycle's Scout brief and approved freeze before dependent writes. Other policy,
compatibility or authority changes receive the matching disposition; if they
alter requirements/acceptance, the same cycle rule applies. A cycle superseded
before assembled freeze consumes no assembled-candidate allowance.

Implementers own L1 inspection of real callers, original data shapes, composed
transitions and affected readers. Focused L2 diagnostics answer concrete
uncertainties within existing authority. Compiles, isolated expressions and
already-normalized substitutes cannot prove an ordinary executing path.

Before assembly, establish critical tool capabilities and producer-to-consumer
compatibility: supported interfaces, actual output names/locations, input/result
bounds and composed failure handling. Reuse still-valid demonstrations. Where
inspection cannot settle a critical assumption, use a bounded permitted check
with disposable nonprivate inputs. This is targeted engineering, not a standing
test suite or a requirement to exercise every path. If the check needs missing
authority, report that dependency; do not silently expand scope.

An unavailable interface, incompatible contract or unestablished capability
needed to execute the selected journey blocks executable readiness and assembly
of that operational packet. Listing it as residual uncertainty does not cure it.
An otherwise reviewable product-source candidate may retain separately gated
operational proof, honestly labeled source-only. Fresh target state, actor
permissions and actual durable results remain operational checks at their proper
boundary. Never bypass a tool policy or repeat a prohibited method as a fallback.

## Assembled Freeze And Candidate Budgets

At assembled freeze, record the exact product/input manifest, complete changed
path inventory, author inspection, current outcome/requirement map, baseline
review references, affected paths and remaining authority. Inspect the finite
evidence envelope against its actual consumers before declaring it assembled.
Keep fresh observations separate from complete toolchain/input identity;
do not replace a required object with a partial observation.

Count a candidate at assembled freeze. Post-freeze product/semantic changes
consume a repair candidate under
[identity and evidence changes](repo-guardrails.md#identity-and-evidence-changes).
Never edit during review. An unchanged-source environment rerun is separately
bounded and needs a changed diagnosis. A new invocation, lane or task does not
replenish any allowance.

Default cumulative limit remains one initial plus two repair candidates;
explicit owner extensions preserve the same program history and consumption.
The Orchestrator sets an effort/time checkpoint before preparation, appropriate
to scope and existing authority; use a 30-minute checkpoint for an unresolved
tooling-feasibility question when none is specified. This is a planning check,
not a new runtime timer or permission to interrupt an operation. At the
checkpoint report the achieved product delta, unresolved assumption and next
bounded action. Continue only with a concrete changed diagnosis inside the
existing effort authority; otherwise return the design choice to the owner.
Do not make the user invent a token estimate or ask again for already granted
routine technical latitude. Unknown spending stays unknown. Respect any
explicit ceiling; at exhaustion preserve work and return the concrete tradeoff.

A failed candidate retains its verdict and complete Frozen Failure Inventory.
Product repair requires the next cycle's Scout/freeze and available matching
allowance; compact tooling successors record the corresponding brief inline.
Classify model, producer, source/data, concurrency, environment and evidence
failures accurately. A second substantive failure in the same supported interface
or contract family after an attempted correction pauses further implementation
of that family for a design/scope disposition. Ordinary draft syntax errors and
review suggestions are mutable feedback, not this trigger. State the common
cause, a simpler supported approach (or why none exists), retained completed
work, critical feasibility evidence, finite next effort and remaining allowance.
Another narrow amendment or renamed candidate alone is not that disposition.
The Orchestrator may resolve technical choices within existing authority; a
changed product, security/preservation policy or exhausted limit needs the owner.
An operational refusal is not an environment rerun or permission to repeat it.

## Independent Review And Reuse

The independent Verifier owns coverage of every current changed path and
affected entry-to-side-effect path. It verifies exact candidate/input identity
before and after review, freshly inspects the successor delta and changed
inputs, and challenges the assembled behavior's applicable access, visibility,
privacy, custody, compatibility, concurrency and failure assumptions.

Previously inspected material may supply coverage only when the Verifier
proves exact bytes and continued validity of the dependency, requirement,
source-policy, audience/permission and environment assumptions relevant to
that claim. Inspect affected siblings and composed paths whenever those
assumptions change; uncertain impact receives fresh inspection. A file being
unchanged is insufficient by itself. New/unreviewed or previously skipped
material needs fresh inspection. The reviewer may broaden scope for a concrete
dependency or risk, recording why it affects the claim; unrelated discoveries
go to the backlog unless they invalidate acceptance or require an immediate stop.

Keep one flat coverage map: current path/digest and requirement or composed
path, fresh or reused disposition, direct original independent-review
reference, current dependency basis, and open findings/skips. Do not nest prior
coverage maps. The author may propose a delta/impact map; the Verifier owns
reuse judgment and cannot adopt author self-approval as independent evidence.

A rejected predecessor supplies no source acceptance. Its independently
completed unaffected inspections may be reused, but every unresolved finding
and its affected dependencies require a fresh disposition. Invalid or skipped
prior analysis supplies no coverage. Exact prospective owner deferrals retain
their scope and historical rejection; they do not waive general privacy,
numeric or state-preservation requirements. Report any dependency-skipped
coverage honestly; it cannot support acceptance.

Acceptance requires complete current coverage, no unresolved blocking finding,
and stated residual uncertainty. Otherwise return one complete Frozen Failure
Inventory, continuing unaffected review after ordinary findings. The reviewer
does not repair the candidate or impose a routine diagnostic gate.

## Complete Release Review

Before protected integration, perform a complete assembled-release review:
fresh inspection of every cumulative changed path and affected
entry-to-side-effect path against the release baseline and exact relevant
inputs. A reuse-only successor review does not satisfy this scope. Release
planning should use the final candidate's review for this complete pass,
rather than scheduling a second review of identical material.

One independent review may satisfy both candidate and complete release coverage
when its exact assembled identity, inputs and whole-release scope match.
Reaching integration alone never requires a duplicate pass. Relevant subsequent
changes invalidate the affected credit and require review under the rules
above; a changed release needs complete coverage for its actual identity.
An integration that changes the reviewed bytes or semantics is not that same
candidate. Full release review is source evidence, not deployment, target-write
authority or proof of a successful native operation.

## Close-Out

Report exact identity, fresh/reused/complete coverage and remaining uncertainty;
actual outcomes and failure class; cumulative consumption; source versus
operational/deployed status; maintenance obligations; remaining side-effect
gates; and retained-lane disposition. Keep raw evidence behind pointers.
Use the existing record and role handoff, not a parallel closure bureaucracy.
The retired automated suite and candidate gate remain unavailable.

## Current-State And Skill Maintenance

When a change alters stable behavior, routing, storage or source authority,
supported commands, or operating procedure, prepare the owning current-state
changes and narrow skill edits within the authorized change, before assembled
candidate freeze. Keep proposed skill edits for unaccepted behavior bound to
that candidate in local evidence and include them in its existing review;
do not publish pending behavior into globally shared skills. After contract
acceptance, apply the reviewed stable shared guidance under existing matching
authorization, without editing the frozen source candidate or adding another
approval/review gate. Correct instructions that already disagree with accepted
facts within the current authorized task; do not defer them to later cleanup.
If guidance is unaffected, one brief close-out statement is enough; do not
manufacture a skill edit.

Use the checked-out source and accepted repository policy to verify the new
wording. Distinguish accepted local or integrated behavior from deployed
behavior. A proposal, stopped candidate, source review, or saved operation
does not establish a shipped capability. Keep candidate IDs, pending verdicts,
repair chronology, and mutable program status in the owning local program
records, not shared skill instructions. Preserve necessary historical evidence
behind explicit references instead of loading it in ordinary task lanes.

Inspect affected callers, reference maps, and examples for superseded commands
or imperatives. Replace or retire the obsolete instruction itself; a new
warning above an incompatible recipe is insufficient. Prefer a link to the
owning contract over another copy of its rules. Change only the affected
specialist references, and leave immutable historical results labeled as history.

Maintaining affected CPW skill documentation is part of an authorized change
when that documentation is within the task's write scope. Reuse matching
authorization rather than asking again. Shared filesystem access alone grants
no additional scope. If a required update is outside that scope, prepare its
exact proposed edit and identify its owner without blocking unrelated work.
Preserve already-frozen authority revisions: a shared guidance update does
not rebind a frozen candidate. Retain its exact bound revision as evidence,
leave its owning worktree unchanged, and apply corrections through the next
authorized successor rather than silently changing its governing rules.

Include changed skill files and cross-references in the change's existing
review. Canonical guidance receives the focused independent semantic review
required by the documentation route; related files can be covered together.
This adds no separate standing test, monitor, or repeated approval gate.

Deliver accepted workflow amendments under
[Instruction Delivery And Reuse](worktrees.md#instruction-delivery-and-reuse).
Close out the actual active consumers and deferred frozen/inactive lanes;
editing main alone is not a completed rollout. Preserve bound historical
authority while giving future assignments an explicit current governance source.
