# Qualification And Outcome Appraisal

Status: prospective protocol for explicitly scoped qualification packets

Use this protocol when preparing a source-stable operation followed by compatible
read-only qualification. It changes the ordering of evidence appraisal, not the
required evidence or permission to execute. Adopt it explicitly in the owning
program's current record and next packet. Existing frozen packets keep their
bound barriers and verdicts; this document cannot release one of their requests.

The [operating model](agent-operating-model.md#proportionate-delivery) owns delivery
classification, independent acceptance and cumulative controls. Preparation
acceptance grants no runtime, character, publication or live-data authority.
This documentation-only revision allocates and consumes no source, helper,
control or runtime candidate; existing explicitly bound accounting remains binding.
An executable adapter or changed operational packet still needs its correct
existing candidate accounting, matching scope and exact independent review.

## Prepare One Coherent Journey

In the existing current record, name the intended real outcome, exact source and
tool/input identity, target and actor, permitted requests/effects, recovery basis,
owned resources, counters/charging boundaries and stopping conditions. Use the
existing packet and review records; introduce no standing dispatcher, receipt
service or extra approval stage.

Group only independently needed surfaces that can use the same unchanged source,
authority and recovery basis. Examples include normal JSON, sheet HTML, an edit
context, rest preview and prospective advancement context. Include a surface only
when it answers a real remaining question. Trace its actual entry-to-side-effect
path: GET and preview labels do not prove absence of session, flash, initialization,
recovery or other writes. Bound the exact permitted incidental effects. A rest
preview is not a rest; an advancement context is not a level-up.

Keep distinct write intents and custody decisions separate. A native checkpoint,
language adoption and item replacement cannot inherit each other's write authority.
Never extend a batch over source edits, changed relevant policy/inputs, unexplained
target drift or a new genuine decision. Reuse valid semantic coverage rather than
repeating it; the independent reviewer establishes continuing validity.

Prepare static comparisons, commands and reviewer availability before issuing an
expiring proposal. Independently preapprove only the exact semantic predicate and
enumerated fresh leaves/cross-bindings. At dispatch, validate the actual fresh
proposal and normal authentication, signature, actor, target, source/state,
candidate, currentness and expiry. Keep every bound time limit: for example, a
packet's 120-second dispatch cap does not replace Source Repair's normal
600-second signature limit. No broad timestamp stripping or token renewal follows.

## Keep Captures; Separate Durable Reconciliation From Full Appraisal

The proposed order for a packet that explicitly adopts this protocol is:

1. Capture complete PRE and verify recoverability and relevant source/target/auth
   identity. Complete the packet's independent readiness appraisal before dispatch.
2. Execute only its actual fresh authorized operation, once. Retain response,
   dispatch, proposal and observer evidence even on refusal, timeout or error.
3. Capture the complete intermediate preservation and durable facts required by
   the packet before another application request. Keep their original bytes and
   identities. This protocol defers full unrelated-data appraisal, not those
   captures, recovery requirements or substantive checks.
4. Independently reconcile the actual durable outcome and relevant safety basis.
   Only the **committed and reconciled** row below can release an already scoped
   dependent read. Record the scoped verdict against the exact packet and captured
   evidence; full preservation, reader qualification and closure remain pending.
5. Complete only the permitted source-stable read set, preserving each actual
   result and observation. Then close owned resources normally, capture full final
   state, and perform one independent combined terminal appraisal.

No intermediate verdict may say that full preservation, reader qualification or
closure passed before those facts have been appraised. A client must enforce the
specific scoped verdict and exact bindings; a generic `accepted: true` or HTTP
status is insufficient. A future client change needs review of that actual
consumer and its durable/final receivers. Existing frozen clients are unchanged.

| Reconciled outcome | Evidence needed | Dependent request disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Committed and reconciled | Exact intended durable definition/import/state and revisions; matching action/proposal/candidate/actor audit and publication; current/admission/mirror/outbox consistency; no relevant pending recovery; explained owned files; unchanged relevant source, policy and access basis | Only the already authorized compatible read set may continue, with normal fresh app guards; full preservation acceptance is still pending |
| Definitely not committed | Exact prior durable state and complete relevant absence/noncommit evidence, without pending or ambiguous publication/recovery | Stop reads dependent on a save. An independently needed read may continue only if the packet explicitly scoped that branch and its safety is proved |
| Committed but reconciliation incomplete | Commit exists but mirror/outbox, recovery, target, observer or other required consistency is unresolved | Stop dependent requests; preserve and reconcile only within separately available authority |
| Unknown or conflicting | Missing capture, timeout, inconsistent evidence, incomplete observer record or failure to establish the actual durable outcome | Stop dependent requests. Preserve uncertainty; no automatic Save/Apply retry, replay, recovery write or new request follows |

A success response is not commit proof; an error response is not noncommit proof.
A known commit must never be replayed because its confirmation or later reader
failed. A final preservation failure likewise cannot erase the actual commit or
be relabeled as a no-effect attempt. Retain each verdict and actual consumption.

## Conditions For Moving The Saved-Read Barrier

Before releasing a read ahead of full preservation appraisal, the independent
review must establish all of the following for that exact future packet:

- The response and complete passive observation are available, the actual intended
  commit is independently reconciled, and no dependent uncertainty remains.
- The read's complete relevant input and access basis is known. Fresh comparison
  covers actor/session validity, roles/membership/assignment, source/policy/config,
  exact target/state and every other dependency affecting safe dispatch; normal
  app authentication and guards still run. Existing valid PRE alone is insufficient.
- The read cannot trigger another consequential operation, initialization or
  recovery under the reconciled state. Its exact incidental session/flash effects
  are bounded and preserved. Required captures and observer health cannot fail
  open. No unreviewed redirect, follow-up getter or fallback is added.
- Deferring appraisal of other data cannot make this read unsafe or obscure its
  outcome. Full intermediate and final evidence remains available, with coherent
  capture boundaries and explicit ownership. If dependency/capture coherence is
  unproved, or a binding guarantee requires full appraisal first, retain the old
  barrier. An estimated speed gain cannot waive it.

This is a conditional ordering choice for future packets, not a general permission
to read after a write. Keep freshness/proof/CAS and independent reserved
reconstruction at their original application boundaries. No source/authority
cache or preflight-to-reservation reuse follows from batching evidence work.

## Combined Terminal Appraisal

The independent terminal reviewer accounts for the complete PRE-to-final delta,
using the intermediate capture and already accepted durable reconciliation as
direct evidence. Review pending preservation and saved-reader/closure facts once;
reuse prior coverage only when its relevant inputs and assumptions remain valid.

Keep the complete schema and every table's columns, typed cell multisets and
duplicate counts, including null/type distinctions; all campaign files and
specifically explained additions/removals; original import/state/history and
unrelated characters; access, source, assignments and recovery evidence. Selected
character totals, row counts, aggregate hashes or a session-only final delta do
not replace that full comparison. Prove the allowed action delta and each later
incidental effect. Compare raw durable bytes with raw reader facts and projections
with their actual projection contract, rather than conflating representations.

Account for each request, refused/failed branch, observation and actual counter.
Confirm the original host exited, every owned socket/lease/observer/auth transport
closed as required, and no unaccounted pending operation remains. Preserve the
provenance of process-exit evidence; do not invent an independent OS query.
Cleanup escalation, force-kill, credential revocation and recovery retain their
separate authority. If normal closure or complete final capture is unavailable,
report that exact uncertainty instead of claiming terminal acceptance.

State separate conclusions for durable outcome, preservation, read qualification,
owned closure and performance. A qualified read cannot cure a preservation defect;
a known commit can coexist with a failed read. Full acceptance requires every
applicable guarantee and no unresolved blocker. Keep full final preservation even
when the requested definition delta is narrow.

## Measurement And Next-Character Preparation

Measure a source change on its real executing path. An ordinary item Review can
observe preparation/readiness without authorizing Apply, but only when a genuine
intended item decision exists. Saved API/form/advancement reads do not exercise an
item normalizer merely because they use the same source tree. Do not replay a
completed relink, manufacture a no-op repair, edit a character for timing, or
invent gameplay. If no real intended operation exists, keep performance unmeasured.

Use existing request timestamps/metrics and required captures for a concrete
question. Separate measured request time from capture, coordination, review and
unknown idle windows. Capped query counters are lower bounds; repeated output
frames do not establish reusable authority or exclusive cost. Different source
versions/operations are not a controlled benchmark. Additional instrumentation or
requests need their own scoped review and allocation.

After the improvement pass, next-character preparation can review existing source
material and retained evidence, identify original-versus-native field custody and
real unresolved owner choices, and draft one coherent supported journey with its
reader/preview/save siblings. Keep that preparation read-only unless further work
is authorized. Do not create another campaign/worktree copy, probe or change the
target, mint a proposal, start a host, or consume a character attempt merely to
prepare the plan. Settle genuine decisions and source feasibility before freezing
the next operational packet; allocate its actual effects and counters explicitly.

Deliver this protocol to active future consumers through the existing current
record. Mark frozen and inactive consumers deferred until they explicitly read
the selected governance on reuse, under
[instruction delivery](worktrees.md#instruction-delivery-and-reuse). Adoption does
not rebind historical candidates, replenish budgets or authorize the next character.
